## 1. Blueprint rules

- This document is an editorial production contract, not article copy.
- The future page owns the broad “baby memory book” intent. Supporting clusters own deep prompts, templates, milestones, photo-book, scrapbook, and digital-product intent.
- Every factual claim must have a source record before approval.
- Developmental content must remain descriptive and non-diagnostic.
- Privacy, retention, backup, export, and AI claims must match the production product at publication time.
- H1 is unique. H2 defines user decision stages. H3 defines answer blocks. H4 is reserved for grouped monthly stages. H5 and H6 are prohibited for this page.
- The final visible FAQ contains 8–10 questions; the complete 47-question map is an editorial demand inventory, not permission to publish all questions.

## 2. Page-level content objective

Enable a parent to understand, choose, plan, begin, and safely preserve a meaningful baby memory book without pressure, while showing how a living digital timeline can complement physical keepsakes.

- [ ] No section exists only to increase length.
- [ ] No competitor text, outline, or examples are rewritten.
- [ ] Interactive blocks enhance rather than hide essential answers.

### E-E-A-T

- [ ] Named author and first-hand parent/editor contribution exist.
- [ ] Milestone content has qualified review and primary sources.
- [ ] Privacy claims have professional review and match production.
- [ ] AI and product claims are verified by product/engineering owners.
- [ ] Sources, corrections, disclosures, and substantive update dates are visible.

### Search intent

- [ ] Broad definition, comparison, planning, trust, and action intents are satisfied.
- [ ] Product choice does not overwhelm informational intent.
- [ ] Final CTA follows, rather than interrupts, decision confidence.

- [ ] Baby Journal owns writing-habit depth.
- [ ] Digital Baby Book owns digital feature/privacy comparison depth.
- [ ] Milestones owns detailed milestone coverage.
- [ ] Photo Book and Scrapbook own production/layout depth.
- [ ] Checklist and Templates own downloadable/action intent.

### Conversion flow

- [ ] No aggressive CTA appears before useful value.
- [ ] Premium appears only after relevant media/preservation context.
- [ ] Privacy, safety, FAQ, and source sections remain sales-neutral.
- [ ] Every CTA target exists and accurately represents the next screen.

- [ ] One H1; 24 H2; planned H3 hierarchy; no skipped heading levels.
- [ ] H5/H6 remain unused.
- [ ] Tables have headers and responsive alternatives.
- [ ] Interactive components have semantic fallbacks and keyboard operation.
- [ ] Paragraphs remain concise; jargon is defined or removed.

- [ ] Privacy lifecycle and preservation are concrete.
